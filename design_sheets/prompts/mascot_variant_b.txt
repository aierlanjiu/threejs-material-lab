Character design sheet for "HUAN / 幻", an adorable collectible 3D mascot companion based on a fresh lychee fruit. 3:2 landscape concept design board.
MASCOT CHARACTER & PEEL DESIGN (Balanced 55% Peel & 45% Pulp):
A lovable designer art toy mascot. The iconic carmine-red / rosy-pink bumpy lychee peel is designed as an organic STANDING CRADLE COLLAR and protective jacket wrapping around the back and lower body (55% of character). The shell shows rich, realistic polygonal lychee rind scales/tubercles, opening naturally with curved peeled petal-like lobes at the neckline and sides, revealing the pale-pink inner rind membrane.
PULP CHARACTER: Nestled inside the textured lychee shell is the chubby, succulent, milky-white lychee fruit flesh (aril) head and upper torso (45% of character). Ultra-smooth, plump, softly translucent with natural organic fruit lobes and gentle moist sheen. Two glossy black oval eyes and an adorable warm smile are set directly in the glowing milky pulp face. At the upper back of the peel collar perches a delicate natural brown twig and ONE small fresh green lychee leaf.
SHEET LAYOUT: Warm off-white minimal studio background, refined editorial mascot design sheet.
1. Upper-left: Large 3/4 hero mascot pose, cute plump posture, showcasing the harmonious dialogue between the structured carmine peel cradle and the soft translucent milky pulp character.
2. Upper-right: Circular macro closeup highlighting the intricate polygonal tubercles of the red peel and the succulent waxy translucency of the fruit pulp.
3. Bottom row: Four distinct full-body mascot expressions labeled "JOY" (crescent smiling eyes), "CURIOUS" (tilted head looking up), "SPEAK" (alert open little 'o' mouth), "SLEEP" (peacefully curled into the peel cradle).
Typography "HUAN · LYCHEE MASCOT / CONCEPT B: PEEL CRADLE" in upper-left. High-end IP mascot collectible design, 65mm product photography, unmistakable lychee identity.
