Industrial character design sheet for "HUAN / 05 · FROSTED GLASS", an anthropomorphic 3D desktop companion pet inspired by a fresh peeled lychee fruit. 3:2 landscape concept board.
CANONICAL CHARACTER FORM: Plump peeled lychee aril form interpreted in velvety frosted semi-translucent glass. The succulent pulp body is sculpted from silky matte milky-white frosted glass with a warm glowing diffuse core, evoking the frosty freshness of a chilled lychee fruit. The surface has a velvety misted tactile finish with gentle organic lobes. Tiny embossed frosted cranberry-pink glass peel accent (<10%) at the lower back edge with delicate crystalline texture. Slender frosted amber-brown glass stem and ONE small narrow frosted olive-green glass leaf at top-back. Face features two deep glossy black inset glass bead eyes and a tiny sculpted mouth set directly into the frosted glass pulp. Ethereal, modern Scandinavian glass art collectible.
